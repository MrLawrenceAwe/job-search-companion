Checking

Checks begin after a description stays selected for 1.5 seconds. One job runs at a time; recent selections take priority. Checks already running finish when you move on. Up to ten waiting jobs are retained. Hidden tabs do not start automatic checks.

Saved findings

Completed findings are saved locally for up to 30 days, with a maximum of 300 results. A changed description, profile, model or checker version requires a new check. Card badges from earlier visits say “Previously checked” until the description is verified again.

Job description text and relevant profile facts are sent to OpenAI using your ChatGPT plan. OAuth credentials stay in the local bridge. No unopened job descriptions are fetched. Findings do not automatically mark jobs unsuitable or recommend a CV.
